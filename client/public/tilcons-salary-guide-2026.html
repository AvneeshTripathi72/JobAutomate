Insights

Key hiring trends, 2026

What every employer and job seeker needs to know as the year progresses.

01

AI fluency is now a baseline

AI proficiency has moved from differentiator to expectation across every sector. Candidates who can work fluently with AI tools command 18–28% salary premiums.

02

Skills-based hiring is the norm

Degree requirements keep dropping in favour of demonstrated skills, portfolios, and certifications — the widest the talent pool has been in years.

03

Speed still wins the best talent

Top candidates are typically off the market within 7–8 days in 2026. Streamlined, fast-moving hiring processes are now a decisive competitive edge.

04

Hybrid is the default, not the perk

Over 75% of professionals rank hybrid working as a top-3 factor in evaluating a role. Rigid in-office mandates continue to cost employers top talent.

05

Salary expectations keep climbing

Candidates across sectors benchmark against live market data and expect 12–22% increases when switching roles. Underbidding drives higher drop-out rates than ever.

06

DEI remains a real differentiator

Organisations with visible, substantive DEI commitments attract more applications and see stronger offer-acceptance, especially among early-career candidates.

Best practices

7 tips to hire better in 2026

Actionable advice from Tilcons consultants, based on 350+ successful placements.

01

Define the role precisely before posting

Vague job descriptions attract the wrong candidates and waste everyone's time. List must-have vs. nice-to-have skills clearly.

02

Move fast at every stage

Delay a day and you risk losing your best candidate. Aim to give feedback within 24 hours after each interview round.

03

Benchmark your offer before making it

Use this guide to ensure your compensation is at or above market — especially for high-demand, AI-adjacent roles.

04

Sell the role and the company

The best candidates have options. Articulate your culture, growth opportunities, and benefits clearly and compellingly.

05

Think in total compensation

Salary is just one element. Flexible working, learning budgets, ESOPs, and healthcare benefits are major decision factors in 2026.

06

Involve a specialist recruiter early

Access to a pre-screened talent pool cuts your time-to-hire by up to 60% and significantly improves quality of hire.

07

Structure your onboarding

The first 90 days are critical. A structured onboarding programme increases first-year retention by up to 82%.
